Company and customer fit

1. What does Cadre AI do?
Cadre AI helps businesses identify valuable AI opportunities, develop a strategy, implement workflow automations and agents, and prepare employees to use them effectively. Its focus is business outcomes such as revenue growth, profitability, and reduced manual work.

2. What services does Cadre offer?
Cadre offers four core services: AI Strategy, AI Leadership & Facilitation, AI Engineering, and AI Agents.

3. Is Cadre a software product or a consultancy?
Cadre is an AI strategy and implementation consultancy. Its work can include selecting existing tools, connecting systems, automating workflows, and building custom solutions.

4. What kinds of companies are a good fit/does Cadre serve?
B2B companies: professional services, private equity, financial services, real estate, construction, manufacturing, retail, and more

5. Do we need to know exactly what AI solution we want?
No. Cadre’s approach includes discovering opportunities across departments and identifying where AI could create meaningful business value.

6. Will Cadre always build custom software?
No. Cadre considers capabilities in existing tools and available products before choosing custom development.

Sources for 1-6: [Cadre homepage](https://cadre.ai/), [AI Strategy](https://cadre.ai/strategy), [AI Engineering](https://cadre.ai/ai-engineering).

Services and engagement approach

7. What does the AI Strategy service involve?
It identifies business problems across departments, assesses potential ROI and feasibility, and prioritizes opportunities for implementation.

8. What are Cadre’s eight pillars of AI transformation?
They cover a dedicated AI team, an AI command center, culture change, an enabled technology stack, data readiness, agent readiness, departmental deep dives, and a three-year AI vision.

Sources for 7-8: [AI Strategy](https://cadre.ai/strategy)

9. What is the AI Transformation Intensive?
It is a structured engagement in which Cadre and your leadership team develop an AI transformation strategy together.

10. How long does the Intensive take?
The published Intensive runs from kickoff to a twelve-month roadmap within 45 days. Confirm the applicable scope and schedule with Cadre.

11. What does the Intensive include?
The published sequence includes kickoff, the AI Maturity Index, a full-day workshop, a use-case library, a three-year vision, and a twelve-month roadmap.

12. Does the 45-day Intensive mean every AI solution will be deployed in 45 days?
The published timeline describes the Intensive through delivery of its roadmap. It should not be interpreted as a delivery guarantee for every subsequent implementation.

Sources for 9-12: [AI Transformation Intensive](https://cadre.ai/ai-transformation-intensive)

13. What is AI Leadership & Facilitation?
Cadre runs experiential sessions combining technical implementation experience, behavioral science, and executive coaching to help leaders and teams adopt AI.

14. What workshop formats are available?
The website lists a one-hour virtual kickoff, half-day executive session, one-day workshop, and two-day leadership intensive.

15. What do teams leave a facilitation session with?
The service describes identifying three to five specific business problems, prioritizing opportunities, and developing actionable next steps.

16. Can Cadre help employees who are skeptical or worried about AI?
Yes. Its facilitation work explicitly addresses resistance, uncertainty, employee concerns, and leadership alignment.

Sources for 13-16: [AI Leadership & Facilitation](https://cadre.ai/leadership-facilitation)

17. What does AI Engineering cover?
Cadre connects systems and automates workflows using existing products, integrations, and custom AI where appropriate. Examples include document routing, email triage, data entry, and reporting.

18. Does Cadre build AI agents and voice agents?
Yes. Cadre describes services ranging from prompts and assistants to agents that act across tools with guardrails and human oversight. Voice-agent examples include intake, qualification, support, and call routing.

Sources for 17-18: [AI Engineering](https://cadre.ai/ai-engineering), [AI Agents](https://cadre.ai/agents)

AI Maturity Index

19. What is the AI Maturity Index?
It is a company-wide survey and report showing AI readiness, adoption, strengths, and areas needing attention.

20. Is it free?
Yes. The website says the survey, portal, and results are free, without an obligation to purchase services.

21. Who should participate?
Cadre encourages everyone in the company to participate so the results represent different departments.

22. How long does it take?
About ten minutes per person. The published survey window is two weeks.

23. How do we get started?
Use the AI Maturity Index page. Its form asks for company name, company website, your name, and work email.

24. What happens after registration?
The page says Cadre emails a code to set up your portal.

25. Can the whole team use one survey link?
Yes. The site describes sharing one link across the organization.

26. What does the report include?
An overall score and maturity stage, departmental comparisons, readiness areas, requested use cases, and employee concerns.

27. What are the maturity stages?
Unaware, Exploring, Experimenting, Operationalizing, and Scaling.

28. What happens after we receive our results?
You can use them independently or discuss a prioritized 90-day plan with Cadre.

Sources for FAQs 19–28: [AI Maturity Index](https://cadre.ai/ai-maturity-index)
